# 2009 AMC 12B Problem 3

> This problem is the same as [2009 AMC 10B #5](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2009_AMC_10B_Problems) and [2009 AMC 12B #3](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2009_AMC_12B_Problems).
## Problem
Twenty percent less than 60 is one-third more than what number?


$$\mathrm{(A)}\ 16\qquad \mathrm{(B)}\ 30\qquad \mathrm{(C)}\ 32\qquad \mathrm{(D)}\ 36\qquad \mathrm{(E)}\ 48$$

## Solution
Twenty percent less than 60 is $\frac 45 \cdot 60 = 48.$ One-third more than a number *n* is $\frac 43$n. Therefore $\frac 43$n = 48 and the number is $\boxed$ {36}. The answer is $\mathrm{(D)}.$
