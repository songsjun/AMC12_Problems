==Problem==
All sides of the [[convex polygon|convex]] [[pentagon]] ABCDE are of equal length, and \angle A = \angle B = 90^{\circ}. What is the degree measure of \angle E? 

\mathrm {(A)}\ 90 \qquad \mathrm {(B)}\ 108 \qquad \mathrm {(C)}\ 120 \qquad \mathrm {(D)}\ 144 \qquad \mathrm {(E)}\ 150

==Solution==
[[Image:2007_12B_AMC-7.png]]

Since A and B are [[right angle]]s, and AE equals BC, and AECB is a [[square]]. Since the length of ED and CD are also 5, triangle CDE is [[equilateral triangle|equilateral]]. Angle E is therefore 90+60=150 \Rightarrow \mathrm {(E)}
