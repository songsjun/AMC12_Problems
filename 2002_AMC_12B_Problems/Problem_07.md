# 2002 AMC 12B Problem 7

> This problem is the same as [2002 AMC 12B #7](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2002_AMC_12B_Problems) and [2002 AMC 10B #11](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2002_AMC_10B_Problems).
## Problem
The product of three consecutive positive integers is 8 times their sum. What is the sum of their [squares](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/perfect_square)?


$$\mathrm{(A)}\ 50 \qquad\mathrm{(B)}\ 77 \qquad\mathrm{(C)}\ 110 \qquad\mathrm{(D)}\ 149 \qquad\mathrm{(E)}\ 194$$

## Solution
Let the three consecutive positive integers be $a-1$, a, and $a+1$. Since the mean is a, the sum of the integers is $3a$. So 8 times the sum is just $24a$. With this, we now know that a$(a-1)(a+1)=$2$4a\Rightarrow(a-1)(a+1)=24.  24=4\times6,$ so $a=5$. Hence, the sum of the squares is $4^2+5^2+6^2=\boxed{\mathrm{ (B)}\ 77}.$
