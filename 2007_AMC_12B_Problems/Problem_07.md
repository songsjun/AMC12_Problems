# 2007 AMC 12B Problem 7

## Problem
All sides of the [convex](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/convex_polygon) [pentagon](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/pentagon) ABCDE are of equal length, and $\angle A = \angle B = 90^{\circ}.$ What is the degree measure of $\angle E$?


$$\mathrm {(A)}\ 90 \qquad \mathrm {(B)}\ 108 \qquad \mathrm {(C)}\ 120 \qquad \mathrm {(D)}\ 144 \qquad \mathrm {(E)}\ 150$$

## Solution
[Image:2007_$12B_AMC-7$.png](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Image:2007_12B_AMC-7.png)

Since A and B are [right angle](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/right_angle)s, and AE equals BC, and AECB is a [square](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/square). Since the length of ED and CD are also 5, triangle CDE is [equilateral](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/equilateral_triangle). Angle E is therefore $90+60=150\Rightarrow \mathrm$ {(E)}
