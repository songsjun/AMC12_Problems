# 2026 AMC 12A Problem 1

> This problem is the same as [2026 AMC 10A #2](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2026_AMC_10A_Problems/Problem_2) and [2026 AMC 12A #1](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2026_AMC_12A_Problems/Problem_1).

## Problem
A sign in a parking lot states that the total price includes a $27$% tax. To the nearest whole number, what percentage of the amount a customer pays is this tax?

$\textbf{(A)}~21\% \qquad \textbf{(B)}~22\% \qquad \textbf{(C)}~27\% \qquad \textbf{(D)}~37\% \qquad \textbf{(E)}~73\%$

## Solution 1
Let the original ticket be $\$100$. Then the tax would be $\$100 \cdot 27\% = \$27$. The customer pays $100+27=\$127$, so the percentage is $\frac{27}{127}=\fbox{\textbf{(A)}\ 21\%}$.

## Solution 2
Let $a$ represent the cost of the ticket. Then, the total cost (with tax) is $\frac{127}{100} a$. The tax is $\frac{27}{100}a$. Thus tax takes up $\frac{27}{127}$. Through long division to three decimals, this is 0.213, or 21.3%, and so the answer is $\fbox{\textbf{(A)}\ 21\%}$.
Note: in this problem, don't take the shortcut by estimating unless you're really good at it.
