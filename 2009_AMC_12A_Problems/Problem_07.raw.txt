== Problem ==
The first three terms of an arithmetic sequence are 2x - 3, 5x - 11, and 3x + 1 respectively. The nth term of the sequence is 2009. What is n?

\textbf{(A)}\ 255 \qquad \textbf{(B)}\ 502 \qquad \textbf{(C)}\ 1004 \qquad \textbf{(D)}\ 1506 \qquad \textbf{(E)}\ 8037

== Solution ==

As this is an arithmetic sequence, the difference must be constant: (5x-11) - (2x-3) = (3x+1) - (5x-11). This solves to x=4. The first three terms then are 5, 9, and 13. In general, the nth term is 1+4n. Solving 1+4n=2009, we get n=\boxed{502}.
