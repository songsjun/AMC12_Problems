# 2008 AMC 12A Problem 3

> This problem is the same as [2008 AMC 12A #3](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2008_AMC_12A_Problems) and [2008 AMC 10A #4](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2008_AMC_10A_Problems/Problem_4).
## Problem
Suppose that $\tfrac{2}{3}$ of 10 bananas are worth as much as 8 oranges. How many oranges are worth as much as $\tfrac{1}{2}$ of 5 bananas?


$$\mathrm{(A)}\ 2\qquad\mathrm{(B)}\ \frac{5}{2}\qquad\mathrm{(C)}\ 3\qquad\mathrm{(D)}\ \frac{7}{2}\qquad\mathrm{(E)}\ 4$$

## Solution
If $\frac{2}{3}\cdot1$0\ $\text{bananas}=$8\ $\text{oranges},$ then $\frac{1}{2}\cdot5$\ $\text{bananas}=\left(\frac{1}{2}\cdot $5\ $\text{bananas}\right)\cdot\left(\frac{8\ \text{oranges}}{\frac{2}{3}\cdot10\ \text{bananas}}\right)=$3\ $\text{oranges}\Longrightarrow\mathrm{(C)}.$
